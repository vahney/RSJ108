Command + Create: MacBook Shortcuts Made Easy

Assembly time: 5-10 minutes
Awesome rating: 95% awesome

Want to make using your MacBook easier? Whether you're writing an article or working on a creative project, keyboard shortcuts can save time. This guide will teach you eight simple MacBook commands that every journalism student should know. All you need is your MacBook and a few minutes to practice!

  • Things You'll Need

  • MacBook or Mac computer
  • Working keyboard
  • Text editor (Google Docs, Word, or Pages)
  • Internet access (optional)

  1. Copy Text (Command + C)

    Highlight the text you want to copy. Hold down the Command (⌘) key and press C. Your selected text will be copied so you can paste it somewhere else.

  2. Paste Text (Command + V)

    Click where you want your copied text to appear. Hold down the Command (⌘) key and press V. Your copied text will appear in that location.

    Use Command + V to quickly paste copied text without having to right-click.

  3. Undo an Action (Command + Z)

    If you accidentally delete something or make a mistake, hold down Command (⌘) and press Z. This will undo your last action.

  4. Redo an Action (Command + Shift + Z)

    If you undo something by mistake, hold down Command (⌘) and Shift, then press Z. This will redo your previous action.

  5. Save Your Work (Command + S)

    While working on a document, hold down Command (⌘) and press S. This will save your work or open a window asking where you want to save it.

  6. Find a Word (Command + F)

    Open a document or webpage and hold down Command (⌘) and press F. A search bar will appear where you can type a word or phrase you want to find.

    Use Command + F to quickly search for specific words or phrases while working on your MacBook. Photo from Flickr.

  7. Take a Screenshot (Command + Shift + 4)

    Hold down Command (⌘) and Shift, then press 4. Your cursor will change into a crosshair. Click and drag to select the part of your screen you want to capture, then release to take the screenshot.

  8. Make Text Bold (Command + B)

    Highlight the text you want to make bold. Hold down Command (⌘) and press B. Your selected text will become bold in applications that support text formatting.
